#### WR-604. `sm_CreateCoonsSrf`'s mean corner twist tilts a fillet corner patch off a face it meets, so the patch crosses the face
- **Severity:** Wrong result (a corner patch 0.0193 to 0.230 past the face it should meet tangentially at r 1 to 7; 26 to 39 degrees off two planar faces at an L-shaped box's mixed corner)
- **Area:** NURBS (B-spline surface corner blends)
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmBSplineSurfaceNLib.cpp`, `sm_CreateCoonsSrf` (static), the `T00`/`T10`/`T11`/`T01` corner twists (`N_VectorCombine( 0.5, CD1[1], 0.5, CD2[1], ...)`), reached from `SmFilletCorner::CreateCornerPatch` through `SmBSplineSurface::CreateCornerBlend` (four-sided, not bilinear) and `CreateCoonsPatch`.
- **What happens:** the bicubic Coons patch takes each corner's twist as the mean of the twists of the two cross-boundary derivative curves meeting there. A side meets its cross derivative only when the corner's twist is the other side's: the patch's cross derivative along a side is that side's derivative curve plus the Hermite blend of (other side's twist less the corner twist) at its two ends. Each twist's part along the normal is its own surface's curvature on the two boundary tangents, so where one side lies on a face of the body and the other on a blend, the mean tilts the patch off the face along the face side, and the patch, tangent to the face only at the corners, crosses it inside.
- **Trigger:**
  - SolidCraft's test-peg part: a bar of stadium section running tangent into a cylinder standing on its round end, the concave arc where the bar's top meets the wall and the bar's front top edge rounded together (a 3x2 mixed-convexity corner). The patch stood past the front face by 0.0193 / 0.0374 / 0.0602 / 0.0900 / 0.128 / 0.174 / 0.230 at r = 1..7. At the corner on the wall, the concave round's side has twist (0, 5.657, 0) and the wall's side 0; the mean tilts the cross derivative by up to 3.9 degrees (issue #1836).
  - An L-shaped box (20 x 20 x 10 less x 10..20, y 10..20) with the notch's concave vertical edge and one convex top edge at (10, 10, 10) rounded r 2 (also a 3x2 mixed corner): the patch meets its two planar faces at 26.2 and 38.6 degrees.
- **How found:** SolidCraft (issue #1836, jyviko). The fork's fix is tangency-labs/smlib@7fd9fb0 (branch peg-corner-bulge, "fillets: a corner patch meets a face of the body with the face's twist"), not merged upstream. Its known limit: at r 5..7 the peg keeps a smaller crossing (0.0016 to 0.034) from a second cause (the convex round side's cross derivative too steep for the patch's width, through `SmTangentField::EvaluatePoint`'s cosine blend), not fixed.
- **Confirmed:** no C++ run was made here (the fork's change is not in the pinned C++). The Rust port follows the pinned C++ (`3fd5b95`) by default.
- **Rust port:** behaves as upstream by default. With `Kernel::fixes.coons_corner_twist_from_blend_side`, `FilletCorner::create_corner_patch_default_with` marks the ordered corner edges with an original face (bit `k` for ordered edge `k`) and passes them through `BSplineSurface::create_corner_blend_with_face_sides` and `create_coons_patch_with_face_sides` to `create_coons_srf`, where a corner between exactly one face side and a blend side takes the blend side's twist (the fork's `sm_FaceSideTwist`); corners between two blends or two faces keep the mean.

  Tests: `tests/issue_1836_coons_corner_twist.rs` (the L-shaped box's corner: both planar seams within 1e-3 degrees with the flag at r 1, 2 and 3, above 20 degrees by default) and `coons_face_side_takes_blend_twist` in `nurbs::b_spline_surface_n_lib::p1` (the face side meets its cross derivative exactly). The test-peg trigger itself fails 1001 in the port with and without the flag: it needs the fork's 3x2 tangent-side-edge fixes (WR-597, WR-600), not ported yet.
